Our Solar System

A journey through eight planets

Mercury

  • Closest to the Sun
  • Smallest planet
  • Extreme temperature swings

Venus

  • Hottest planet
  • Rotates backwards
  • Dense toxic atmosphere

Earth

  • Only known habitable planet
  • One natural satellite (Moon)
  • Liquid water on surface

Mars

  • The Red Planet (iron oxide)
  • Two small moons
  • Evidence of past water

Jupiter

  • Largest planet by far
  • Great Red Spot storm
  • 95 known moons

Saturn

  • Spectacular ring system
  • Second largest planet
  • 146 known moons

Uranus

  • Rotates on its side
  • Ice giant with methane
  • 27 known moons

Neptune

  • Farthest from the Sun
  • Strongest winds in solar system
  • 14 known moons
