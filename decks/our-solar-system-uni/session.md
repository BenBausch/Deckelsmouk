## About

- Deck about the University of Luxembourg: its three missions (education, research, contribution to society),
  its three faculties (FSTM, FDEF, FHSE), its three interdisciplinary centres (LCSB, SnT, C²DH) and its campuses.
  15 slides; folder name ("our-solar-system-uni") is left over from the deck's creation.

## Decisions and preferences

- Built on the Uni.lu PowerPoint template: dark-blue title slide, light-blue section slides, red-dot content
  slides, red-dot thank-you slide. Content slides use custom `.pillar` cards, `.units` bullet lists and
  `.figure` key figures defined in `#deck-style` (section "This deck").
- Institution slides follow one pattern: title, one-line lead, a wide card with departments/focus (step 1),
  a narrow card with three key figures (step 2).

- Audience is the general public at an outreach event: plain words, acronyms demoted to the small card tag with
  a plain-language name above, one idea per slide, lively motion (cards "pop" in staggered rows).
- Slide 6 "One university, six houses" is the pattern for that: a labelled row of three faculties, then a
  labelled row of three centres.

## Notes

- Figures (~6,700 students, 130+ nationalities, ~60 SnT partners) are rounded and from memory — verify on
  uni.lu before presenting.
