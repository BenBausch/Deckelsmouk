Three missions

Education · Research · Contribution to society

What the University is for, before we look at how it is organised.

Three missions, one university

Mission 1

Education

Bachelor, master and doctoral programmes taught by the researchers who make the field, in three languages and with a semester abroad built into every bachelor.

Mission 2

Research

Research concentrated in a few strong fields — digital transformation, medicine and health, European law and finance, education and society — across faculties and interdisciplinary centres.

Mission 3

Contribution to society

Knowledge and technology transfer, spin-offs, advice to public institutions and companies, and lifelong learning for the country.

Three missions laid down in the law that created the University; everything that follows serves them.
